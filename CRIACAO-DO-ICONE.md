# Criação do ícone

Ferramenta: ImageGen integrada, com auxílio de IA.

Pedido final: flor de girassol com pétalas e miolo, sem caule nem folhas, fundo branco, aparência vetorial em 8 bits. O resultado foi redimensionado para 128 × 128 pixels, preservando as bordas pixeladas.

Prompt final usado:

Edit the sunflower icon just generated. Keep its yellow petals and dark brown center, but REMOVE the entire stem and ALL green leaves. The output is ONLY the sunflower flower head, large and centered, front view, circular radial arrangement of yellow petals around a dark brown disk. Crisp clean 8-bit pixel-art silhouette with square stair-stepped edges and simple flat vector-like solid colors. Pure WHITE background #FFFFFF across the entire square. No gradients, no shading, no texture, no border, no text. Balanced white margin of approximately 10% around the flower. Designed to read clearly as a 128x128 extension icon. Preserve the recognizable sunflower appearance. It must have no stem, no leaves, no green parts.
